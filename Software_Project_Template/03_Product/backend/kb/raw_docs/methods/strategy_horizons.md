# Strategy Horizons

A short horizon emphasizes near-term uncertainty, liquidity needs, and sensitivity to market events. Price moves over short periods can be dominated by news, positioning, and noise.

A medium horizon allows more time for operating results and valuation changes to become visible, while still carrying meaningful timing risk.

A long horizon can reduce the importance of individual daily moves, but it does not remove business, valuation, concentration, or market risk. Long-term scenarios should define what evidence will be reviewed over time.

Educational scenarios are not forecasts. They should include a base case, an adverse case, and clear conditions that would change the interpretation.
