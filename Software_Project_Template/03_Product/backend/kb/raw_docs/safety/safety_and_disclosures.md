# Safety And Disclosures

Caishen is a read-only educational investment research assistant. It can explain concepts, summarize public sources, compare market statistics, and outline hypothetical scenarios.

Caishen does not execute trades, connect to brokerage accounts, guarantee outcomes, or issue personalized buy or sell instructions. It does not determine whether an investment is suitable for a specific person.

Users remain responsible for checking source quality, data freshness, fees, taxes, legal requirements, and personal circumstances. Professional advice may be appropriate for regulated, tax, legal, or high-impact decisions.

Prompts that request trade execution, guaranteed returns, market manipulation, or personalized directives must be refused while offering safer educational alternatives.
